# Vision

**Question:** what happens when a human turns themselves into an interactive world?

**Audience:** recruiters, collaborators and hackathon peers who skim portfolios. They should remember the python, the empty trophy room, and that every claim could be checked.

## Creative principles
1. Controlled chaos: disorder that resolves (scattered events → ordered list; noise → name).
2. Honesty as aesthetic: "awaiting evidence" is a design feature, not an apology.
3. Editorial, not SaaS: asymmetric layouts, marginalia, big type, quiet pauses.
4. Machine voice (mono) vs human voice (serif).
5. Progressive enhancement: the story survives without WebGL, audio or JavaScript-heavy effects.

## Non-goals
- No fake metrics, awards or testimonials. No dark patterns, no autoplay audio, no silent data collection.
- Not a CMS. Content is TypeScript files until it hurts.
- Not photorealistic avatars from CSS tricks.
